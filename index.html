Frequently Asked Questions

Crazy Farm is a next-generation decentralized Web3 Telegram gaming ecosystem built on the TON blockchain, empowering players to earn cryptocurrency and farm points passively and through fun gameplay.
You can boost your farming speed by upgrading your Bee Farm with points, activating 12-hour Ad farm cycles, or purchasing the 24/7 VIP Dog with TON to auto-collect continuously.
Upcoming updates include NFT crop breeding, peer-to-peer marketplace trading, guild farming tournaments, and TON jetton staking rewards.
Crazy Farm Coin (CFC) is the official utility token of the Crazy Farm ecosystem, backed by real treasury value on TON and convertible from Farm Points at a 1,000 : 1 ratio.
Withdrawals are processed directly on the TON blockchain to your connected non-custodial wallet (such as Tonkeeper or Telegram @wallet) within minutes.
Our smart contracts are verified on TON, liquidity is locked, and all mining math is 100% transparent and deterministic.
We prioritize our player community with weekly TON giveaways, active Telegram governance discussions, and dedicated 24/7 player support.
If you experience any issues connecting your wallet or withdrawing tokens, ensure your Telegram app is updated to the latest version, disconnect and reconnect your wallet, or reach out to our official support bot @CrazyFarmSupportBot.
To withdraw your earnings, go to the Wallet tab, link your non-custodial TON wallet, ensure you have met the minimum threshold of 1,500 CFC, and tap Withdraw. Points conversion to CFC is 100% FREE. Withdrawals have a standard 50 CFC gas fee, but owning the VIP Dog (3 TON) completely eliminates the gas fee (0 CFC fee)!
The Farm Battle Arena (PvP) lets you enter real-time 3D animal combat in 3 tiers: 3 vs 3 (Bronze), 5 vs 5 (Silver), and 7 vs 7 VIP Championship (Gold)! Squads are dynamically formed using the active animals from your garden. Win battles to earn direct CFC crypto tokens, farm point rewards, and climb the global leaderboard!
