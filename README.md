## 🌟 Key Game Features

### 1. 🚜 3D Living Animal Farms
* **9 Unique Animal Farms:** Chickens, Cows, Ducks, Bunnies, Dogs, Sheep, Horses, Pigs, and Legendary Guardians with fluid keyframe animations.
* **12-Hour Mining Cycles:** Continuous passive yield generation with instant harvest mechanisms.

### 2. ⚔️ PvP Battle Arena
* Real-time squad championships (3vs3, 5vs5, and 7vs7).
* Activity-scaled combat power directly calculated from farm level and animal upgrades.

### 3. 🌋 Jurassic Farm (CFT Mining Sanctuary)
* Mythical sanctuary featuring 5 legendary creatures (Dinosaur, Phoenix, Anaconda, Dragon, Dolphin) mining genuine CFT daily.

### 4. 🎁 10-Day Daily Check-in Streak
* Progressive reward scale from 1,000 🌾 up to 250,000 🌾.
* **Day 10 Jackpot:** **+250,000 🌾 & +5 CFT Tokens** (Doubleable to **+500,000 🌾 & +10 CFT** via 2x Ad Boost).

### 5. 💎 Non-Custodial Web3 Wallet
* Native **TonConnect 2.0** integration supporting Tonkeeper, MyTonWallet, OpenMask, and Telegram Wallet.
* Direct on-chain CFT verification, balance tracking, and withdrawal requests.
